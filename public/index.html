Waste Classification & Segregation Guide

Learn how to properly segregate waste at home, identify color-coded bins, and avoid landfill pollution.

Smart Segregation Golden Rules

🚿

Dry & Clean

Always wash food and drink containers. Recyclers reject greasy paper and unwashed plastic.

📦

Flatten Cartons

Flatten cardboard boxes before collection to maximize vehicle load efficiency.

🔋

Never Mix E-Waste

Lithium batteries cause fires in garbage compactors. Always use dedicated e-waste pickups.

⚠️

Hazardous Segregation

Paints, chemical bottles, and pharmaceuticals must be handed over in original sealed containers.
