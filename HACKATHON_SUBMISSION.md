> 🌍 **Problem Statement:**  
> Urban communities struggle with responsible waste disposal, often mixing hazardous and electronic waste with regular garbage. Meanwhile, collection services face major logistical headaches coordinating and tracking pickups.
>
> 💡 **The Solution: EcoCollect**  
> An end-to-end Smart Waste Collection & Recycling Operating System that makes responsible disposal as simple as booking a cab!
>

> ✅ **Measurable Eco-Rewards:** Live calculation of CO₂ prevented, tree-year absorption equivalents, and printable Green Certificates!
